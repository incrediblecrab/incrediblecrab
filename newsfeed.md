## September 10, 2026

**LLMMM** — published the full-catalog ingredient dataset and the demo. The
current public release is
[llmmm-recipes v0.4.0-recipe-search](https://huggingface.co/incrediblecrab/llmmm-recipes),
public and ungated, trained on all 4,653,430 canonical recipe records. The
ingredient predictor is a masked-set Transformer encoder; the release adds
separate recipe-ranking policies.
